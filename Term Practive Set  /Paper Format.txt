Based on the latest official exam patterns, here is the exact, subject-wise format for the CBSE Class 10 board exams. 

For the main subjects (Mathematics, Science, Social Science, English, and Hindi), the total is 100 marks: **80 marks for the theory paper** and **20 marks for internal assessments**. The theory exams are 3 hours long and contain no negative marking. Information Technology (IT) follows a 50-50 theory and practical split.

### 6. Information Technology (IT - Code 402)
**Structure:** 50 Marks Theory | 50 Marks Practical/Internal

**Section-Wise Format (2-Hour Theory Paper)**
The paper features 21 total questions, out of which you must answer 15. 
| Section | Component | Total Marks | Content Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | Objective Questions | 24 | Contains multiple-choice questions based on both employability and subject skills |
| **B** | Subjective Questions| 26 | Includes short, long, and step-based application questions |

**Unit-Wise Contents & Weightage (Theory)**
* **Part A: Employability Skills (10 Marks)** 
  * Communication, Self-Management, ICT, Entrepreneurial, and Green Skills.
* **Part B: Subject-Specific Skills (40 Marks)** 
  * Digital Documentation (Advanced): ~10 Marks
  * Electronic Spreadsheet (Advanced): ~10 Marks
  * Database Management System (Base): ~12 Marks
  * Web Applications and Security: ~8 Marks
